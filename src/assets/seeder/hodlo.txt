EXODUS 1
CHAPTER 1
Israel naabou nang ghou phrey rapoh 
1	Jacobnanh bei rweipungnang jhuckou Egypt nang vaarapu Israel naabouvey ba̲nan. 
2	Reuben, Simeon, Levi, judah, 
3	Issachar Issachar, Zebulun, Benjamin, 
4	Dan, Naptali, Gad, Asher, 
5	Jacob veynaang setrapu mih Ihen than gin, Josepnanh Egyptnang yei mou sashau. 
6	Joseph ka bei chinabou, beivei theifen mou sheenou. 
7	Israel veynaang setrapu theifen kayey reysa̲nye ba̲lih nanh ren chhāye leipāk mou phreydang rinou,
8	Egyptnang divaang gathenpu tha ki raset dou, beinanh Josephnang gas hihchou. 
9	Beinanh bei mih lhaa hwangu, zaangrou, Israel naabounanh alih manang ren gayey ga̲lengnei ngouyeo, 
10	Alihnanh losha̲n naang sanang ra rou, ba̲lihnanh yāngsa̲nye diraapucha ka̲ zwiklo alihcha gachhennei leipāk vey naang zenlen vāu. 
11	Ba̲lih manang zaangrapu va̲ja sa̲nye, baa̲lih lhaa nākanapu ni ghaa ka̲ruebpu gafunye pharaohvey reera̲n lhavāpu keinung, Pi thom ka Raamses keinung sayenou. 
12	Ba̲lihnang ghouphreyulee ba̲lihnanh galeng nei reysanye yāngkou lennei, doupura Israel naabou pāngnaang Egypt mihnanh ninyaa ka̲zwik lākrinnou. 
13	Ba̲lihnanh Israel naabounang sa̲klākra yei gasau. 
14	Dilei nānye chāklun roye, dileivey yei ka̲saye, yei mounang sa̲klākra saye ba̲lihnanh nalākra non nongye ngo-u. 
15	Egypt vangpunanh Hebrew naa ganaabou (midwives) Shiphrah ka, Puah lhaa hwangu. 
16	Nasinanh Hebrew ni foubounang naa ganaanit ranang ngāk kei dang laabou tatou kashirou, ni foubou tatou garanroura hwangu. 
17	Naa ganarapu khininanh shureenang jiye Egypt vangpunanh hwangrapu dlo locha laabou saloulee kashicha raangvapu saneiu.
18	Egypt vangpunanh naa kananit rapu khininang zaalo hwangu, nasinnanh laabounang kashicha chhou ra raangvapu saparou? 
19	Ba̲lihsinnanh hwangkhennou, Hebrew nifoubouku Egypt nifoubou lhenchau, ba̲lihnanh ren ngourāveynanh haasinnee vaapata naang naa salhā rinnnaleiu. 
20	Naa ganaa rapu khini lhaa shureenanh diveípu sani dimih galengsanye ren reysannou.
21	Naa ganaapu khininanh shureenang jiye ba̲lih rweypungnang zaangye saneiu. 
22	Pharaoh nanh dimih mou lhaa kukkong neiu, Hebrew nifoubounanh salo rapu laabou mou Nile chwengnang vāsa̲nrou, nifoubounang sararou.

EXODUS 2
CHAPTER 2
Moses nang sarapoh
1	Levi rweypung mih labou thaki nanh Levi naa nifoubou thakinang yet dou. 
2	Doupu nifoubounanh ka̲ya̲kri zaangshi laabou thaki saloye lhachhuin thweyu.
3	Beinanh yoklākra thweyrā chinnei, naa ka̲yinpunang sa̲nlo chwen khuhnang reengaa rung yaanang vaathweyrinnou. 
4	Nanoobou nanh chhou set teirou rapu theivapu palonanaang zaangye ngou. 
5	Pharaoh naa nifuwunanh bei rwei gararbou nifoubou thakinang jhuekou chwennang dirue praye ra rau, raonanh chwen khuhnang zet sheewuye vaaranaang reengaa yaanang naa kayinpu lotheiye, chhou ba̲ngku vaaloroura beivei raonang gasweyu. 
6	Beinanh naanoobounang lhāngzang rareenang naanoobounanh gararye chwehbou, beinanh prinnei fenglo hey Hebrew bouvey naa ra hwangu.
7	Bei deinanh pharaoh naa nifuwu lhaa hwangu, naanoobounang naarapu Hebrew ni foubounang neelha vanzanine? 
8	Pharaoh naa nifuwunah vanzaroura hwangye beinanh naanoobou yaa-nang vanzau. 
9	Pharaoh naa nifuwunanh beil ha hwangu, naa hey fengkou vannaarou hananh neelhā naa ganaa chengniu. Nifoubounanh naa dou fengkou vannaau. 
10	Naa dou deiye beinanh pharaoh naa nifuwu lhaa jhuekou vaninnou, nit tabou dou pharaoh naa nifuwu naa sanou, beinang hananh dirue veynaang loset doura nittabou nan dou Moses ra beinanh zaaneiu. Moses nanh midiannang vaarapoh
11	Mosesnanh dei koukaye thangy bei mihnanh fungrapu ghaa dou  vaazaangye rareenang Egypt mih thaki nanh bei mih Hebrew mih thakinang vunit rapu vaatheiu. 
12	Zaangaley chhitalee ngouchi rapu theiye beinanh Egypt mihnang sathetlo leinān naang dikhot shinrinnou. 
13	Khinipu naang vaalen naley Hebrew mih khini gavuye ngoualeiu, doupurā beinanh kayweipu mih lhaa hwangu, nang chhoura niphou branang vupārou? 
14	Neenang chhinanh haa manang zaangvapu mihpu sanrarou? Egypt mihnang sathetrā ba̲lengnei nang ananglee sathet ngunne? yei hey theiyetoura Mosesnanh jiu.
15	Chenla dou pharaohnanh ya Mosesnang sathetteira phau. Beinanh pharaoh veynaang zenvaye Midian leipāknang vaazangye rueghong khuhnang vaashun ngou. 
16	Doureenang Midian panmon naa nifoubou thangi zettaleiu, beipuhvey yao veibou lhaa dirue gasueyera dirue gasue vapu ghongnang phreydangra vaasoksa̲nnneiu. 
17	Yao ngākbounanh yao rakhongkou lennou, beinanh thozaangye yao lhaa dirue soksa̲nchau.
18	Ba̲lihnanh vanra reenang sheelung chhoura kalhung naang vanyerou? ra beipuhnanh nau. 
19	Egypt mih thakinanh yao ngākbou kuknaang loye dirue soklo yao veibou lhaa gasueu. 
20	Beipuhnanh beinabou lhaa hwangu, dimih dou kounang ngourou? beinang chhoura venra vanrou, beinang vaazaalo ya nitrou. 
21	Mosenanh beicha maraye ngouye bei naa Zipporahnang gayet dou. 
22	Beinanh naa laabou thaki saloye bei nang Gershom ra zaaneiu, hananh leipak ka thenpunang rabra yiura beinanh zaarāveyud .
23	Baree yoklākra ngoura suenang Egypt vangpu sheenou. Israel cheh nanh doubou lha̲ngnang rao saye nari khanei garwey phaye ba̲rāye gararu, ba̲lih lha̲ngnang rao saye garar rapu shuree ngaa kakennou. 
24	Ba̲lihnanh garar rapu dou shureenanh ya, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, rabou lhaa karinnit rapu dou ba̲ninlo-u.
25	Shureenanh Israel mihnang theiye ba̲lihvey gasā gafung lotheiu.

EXODUS 3
CHAPTER 3
Shuree nanh Mosesnang zaalo rapoh
1	Mosesnanh ba̲gi Midianvey pang mon Jethrovey yao veibou ngākkei zangna̲k zangpu shapa shureevey gafung Horeb ywengphānang yao vangākgou. 
2	Shureevey raonanh beilha gatheiye lono choye meilhey shugnang meilhey settei choulee lono dou chorapu dou beinanh zaangaley chhoutalee  cho dimānchou.
3	Lono chochaley khutam kareipu gathei rapu hey ha vazaang  zangycora Mosesnanh hwangu.  
4	Mosesnanh vazaang rapu dou shuree nanh theiye, lono shungnaang shureenanh Moses ira zaye Mosesnanh ha heynang ngouyeora hwangkhennou.
5	Nangnanh chāngwokpu sa̲nkou heynang arānitrou, nang thozaang rapu phawn hey gathengpu phawn moura shureenanh hwangu. 
6	Hananh neepuhvey shuree, Abraham, Issac, Jacob, rabouvey shuree doura hwangye Mosesnanh shureenang zaangjiye zāk thweynou.
7	Egyptnang ngourapu amihvey nākanapu hananh theiyeo, ba̲lihvey vaja bounanh ghouphreyye karar rapu dou yau, ba̲lihvey nāka̲nāpu hananh theiyeo ra shureenanh hwangu. 
8	Doubounang Egypt leipāk Egypt mih kuknaanglosettei Canaan mih Hittite mih, Amorite mih, Perizzite mih, Hivite mih, Jebusite mih, ngourapu phawn diveipu ma̲si nittei rue ka lāngrue longrapu phawnnang sangou vapu hananh rayeo. 
9	Atuelee Egypt mihnanh ghouphreyye Israel mihnanh ga̲rar rapu tha̲k angā kaye ngouyeo, ba̲lihnang Egypt mihnanh sa̲k lākra ghouphrey rapu hananh theiyeo. 
10	Rarou, amih Israel naabounang Egypt leipāk naang loset vapu, neenang hananh pharaoh ngaa gavayeo. 
11	Mosesnanh hwangkhennou, hananh chhou mih toura pharaoh kuk naang Israel naa boounang Egypt leipāk naang valoset tharou? 
12	Hananh neecha      tasā  vaayeo, khutam hey neelha   pāngu, hananh neenang gavaye amihnang Egypt naang loset rareenang nangnanh heypu kafungnang shuree vey yei sayeo. 
13	Mosesnanh shuree lhaa hwang Israel naabou phawnnang hananh vatatou, napuhvey shureenanh nangaa anang kasweyrāyeo, ra hwangranang,bei nang kararou? ra ba̲lihnanh angā naranang hananh ba̲lih lhaa chhou dlo hwangrou? 
14	Shureenanh Moses lhaa hwangu, hadou ha ngouyeo, Isreal naa bou lhaa hey vaahwang va̲pu hananh neenang ka̲sweyyeo.
15	Israel naabou lhaa hey vaahwangrou napuhvey shuree, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, rabouvey shureenanh nangaa anang kasweyrayeo, anang hey kapehpu ngoucha mih dung changnang angouyanyeo.
16	Israel naabou tengbounang vaazaazwin mei doubou lhaa dlo hey hwangrou, neepuhvey shuree, Abraham, Issac, Jacob, rabou vey shureenanh alha gatheiye hwangu, Egypt leipāknang na manang sarapu yei dou hananh theiyeo. 
17	Egypt nang nāka̲nāpu veynaang nanang losettei Canaan mih, Amorite mih, Rittite mih, Perizaite mih, Hivite mih, Jebusite ngourapu phawn lāngrue ma̲si nittei rue longrapu phawnnang nanang sangouyera hananh karinzatou. 
18	Doubounanh neetlo tla̲nye, nangnanh Israel naabou teibounang jhuekou, Egypt vangpu ngaa vaaye, Hebrew mihvey shuree nanh haachā garoktou haavei shureenang khorum vapu zangna̲k ka̲chhuin naang tareishong vaavapu kukong nitrou. 
19	Egypt vangpu lhaa ren karei pu gathei pātaku nanang diphritchā rapu hananh theiyeo. 
20	Akuk ka̲changye Egypt      leipāknang ba̲jung. gaphā rapu ren gatheiye tanti niu, dousuenangta Egypt vangpunanh nanang diphritteiu. 
21	Egypt mih lhaa ninyaa veivapu hananh gatheini, nananh dounaang setra reenang ka̲tlanta vaachau. 
22	22Nifoubou mounanh rweykhanābou ngaa, parā rangou rabou ngaa, shana taka, Jewel, seeree nanabou laabou nifoubou seeree sani,Egpyt mihvey reeran gasenloyeo.

EXODUS 4
CHAPTER 4
Moses lhaa kuk tweng gathei rapoh 
1	Ba̲linanh anang gaphrācha atlolee tla̲nchau, neelha shuree yo gatheichoura ba̲lihnanh hwangyeo ra Moses nanh hwangu. 
2	Neekuknang chhou logourou? ra shureenanh nau, beinanh gazei logouyco ra hwangu. 
3	Neezei lhalāng gavā lhāroura, hwangye beinanh gazei gavālhā rareenang gazei dou morue gungnou, Mosesnanh dounaang zenvanou. 
4	Neekuk naang ghouloroura shureenanh hwangye beinanh morue meinaang ghouloaley beivei kazei bei kuknang gunglennou, 
5	Heyhrā satatou nafubouvey shuree, Abraham, Issac, Jacob, rabouvey shureenanh neelha gathei rapu dou doubounanh kaprayeo ra hwangu. 
6	Neekuk neechanhnang galānrou ra shureenanh hwanglennei beinanh bei kuk chanhnang galānaley beikuk dou dirān ba̲lengnei ngoopawnta rashinou.
7	Neekuk neechānhnang galānroura hwanglengnei beinanh kuk chānh nang galānzizi losettaley béikuk dou gakhwaavey pewnsuch gungnou, 
8	8Doubounanh neenang kaphrācha ka khuhpu khutamlee kaphrāchi tatou khi ni pu khutammang gaphrāyeo. 
9	9Khutam khinilee gaphrācha neetlolee gaphrachi tatou, nangnanh Nilevey dirue yetlo dilei manang lunglha theirou, chwennang yetlo ravey dirue dou dilei manang diyi gungneiu;
10	Deimei shuree, hananh min ren ngouchou, kakhwaanaang ngouchou, deimeinah neevei rou lhaa hwangra reenanglee ngouchou, lhei    chhālakra gasweita hwangyeo ra Mosesnanh shuree Ihaa hwangu. 
11	Shureenanh hwangu, dimih min chhinanh sanitrou, dlo Iwenmakpu mih, reegong shārapu mih, na̲k theimakopu mih, na̲k theirapu midoubounang chhinanh sanitrou? ha shureenanh ka̲khruitne? 
12	Doupura vaarou, nee minang ha ngouyeo,nangnanh hwangye rapu dlo dou hananh neelha dwengtheiyco ra shureenanh hwangu. 
13	Deimei gathengpu mihnang gasweyrou ra hananh fingneryu ra Mosesnanh hwangu. 
14	Mosesnang Shureenanh lunhshi mei settei hwangu, Levi mih Aaron hey needei ka̲hruitne? beinanh dlo lwenthei rapu hananh theiyeo, beinanh neecha ra garokkeiu, beinanh neenang theira reenang bei ninyaanang kayey marayeo. 
15	Nangnanh    beichā dlo Iwenmei, beinanh lwenvapu neiu, nee min ka bei minnang ha ngouye, nananh saye rapu yei dou hananh dwengtheiyeo. 
16	Beinanh neemin saye, neetlo saye nobou za̲kronnang beinanh hwang yeo. Nanglee beivei shuree ba̲lengnei sayeo.
17	Nangnanh kuknaang logou rapu hey khutam satheirou.
18	Mosesnanh beigi Jethro ngaa ka̲lekkou vanye hwangu, Egyptnang ngourapu haa chehnanh kahrā ngourou rapu ha vaazaangyenanh alha ba̲ree kā ta nitroura fingnei, beigi Jethronanh yueyueta vazangroura hwangu.
19	Shureenanh Moses Ihaa Midianang rahwangu, nangnanh Egypt  leipāknang ga letkou vaarou, neenang sathetteira pharapu mih kamang sheezatou. 
20	Mosesnanh beiyet ka beina khininang sygong tungnang gashunngouye bei kuknaang shureevey gazei dou lokou Egypt leipāk nang vaau. 
21	Shureenanh Moses lhaa hwangu, nangnanh Egyptnang vaazang rareenang hananh neelha ren ni ba̲jung kaphā rapu yei mou pharaoh sānang satheirou, hananh bei ren sachhāye Israel naabounang beinanh diphritchau.
22	Israel naabou hey hananh salo gakhuhpoura shureenanh hwangyeora nangnanh pharaoh lhaa hey vaahwangrou. 
23	Ana bounanh alha yei sanit vapu diphritrou, diphrit nitchara kazitatou neena ba̲zāpu kashiyeora vaahwangroura shureenanh Moses lhaa hwangu.
24	Vaayuch rapu yānang shureenanh Mosesnang theiye beinang satheittei ra phau. 
25	Zipporahnanh raaghata loye beina khini vin zetnit rasa Moses chāng vangtākkei hwangu, ba̲zih naang nang nanh diyi naang adeipuli sarāveyu. 
26	Bei lhengta ngouye beiyetnanh hwangu, ba̲vin zetrapu yei hey nangnanh diyi naang adeipuh saraveyu.
27	Shureenanh Aaron lhaa hwangu,nangnanh Mosescha ywengphānang vaagarokrou, beinanh Mosescha shu reevey gafungnang vaa garokkei bein ang ba̲swetbou.
28	Beinang shureenanh ka̲sweyye khutam sathei vapu sa̲klākra hwangrapu dlo mou Aaron lhaa hwangu. 
29	Moses ka Aaronnanh Israel naabou theicha tasā runkou vaau. 
30	Shureenanh Moses lhaa hwangrapu dlo dou Aaronnanh mou hwanmgye chābou za̲kronnang khutam lee satheiu. 
31	Shuree nanh Israel naabouvey nāka̲nāpu theiye beinanh razaang raveyu ra hwangrapu dlo dou chābounanh ya ba̲lihnanh ko chhuisanye khorum mou.

21	Shureenanh nasinmanang zaang yei bicheree sanitrou, rapu dou pharaoh ka yei sabounanh alihnang ninnei, alih nang sathet vapu nananh sāpāyaeo: 
22	Mosesnanh shuree ngaapa galetkou vanye hwangu, deimei shuree, mih cheh heynang chhoura nangnanh yei sayerou? ananglee chhoura ka̲sweyyerou?

EXODUS 6

Moses nang zaaloraapoh
