Gathengpu Dlo

Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

Gathengpu Dlo is a specialized scripture tool created for the Tangkhul Naga ethnic community of Myanmar. We provide digital access to the Bible in the Ho Dlo, Burmese, and English languages. Our app is built on the principle of transparency and spiritual service.

1. Data Collection Philosophy

We respect your digital boundaries. Gathengpu Dlo does not require user registration, account creation, or any form of "Log in with" services. As a result:

  • We do not collect names, addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not track your specific reading progress on our servers.
  • We do not use your device's camera or microphone.

2. Audio Functionality (Ho Dlo Language)

The app features an integrated audio player exclusively for the Ho Dlo language.

  • Local Playback: Audio files are streamed or downloaded to your device.
  • No Voice Recording: The app never records your voice or background noise.
  • Background Play: The app may request permission to keep the audio running while your screen is off.

3. Necessary Permissions

To function correctly, we may request the following system permissions:

  • Internet: To access Bible text and stream Ho Dlo audio.
  • Notifications: To send you daily verses and reminders.
  • Storage/Media: To allow you to download audio for offline use (essential for areas with poor connectivity).
  • Device State: To pause audio automatically when you receive a phone call.

4. Data Retention and Personalization

Any personalizations you make within the app are stored exclusively on your device's local storage (using SQLite and AsyncStorage). This includes:

  • Reading Progress: Your history of recently read chapters.
  • Favorites & Highlights: Verses you have marked or bookmarked for later study.
  • App Settings: Customizations like font size, font family, and theme (Dark/Light mode).

Because we do not store this data on a cloud server, there is no personal data for us to delete. This information is erased immediately if you uninstall the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Our content is religious and educational. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since the app is anonymous by design, it is a safe environment for users of all ages within the Tangkhul Naga community.

6. Third-Party Integration & Sharing

Analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting tools (such as Google Firebase). Your activity and data remain completely localized to your device.

Sharing Features: When you use the "Share" feature to send a verse to friends or family, the app utilizes your device's native sharing menu. Gathengpu Dlo does not see, store, or transmit information about who you share content with or which platforms you use.

7. Data Security

We take the security of your local data seriously. The app follows standard mobile security protocols to ensure that your bookmarks and reading history are only accessible through the Gathengpu Dlo application on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

As we continue to improve the app, we may update this policy. Any significant changes will be communicated through an in-app notification or an update note in the app store.
